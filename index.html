Guelph Bike Parking

About this map

Where to lock your bike in Guelph, Ontario: bike racks, covered and indoor parking, lockers and shelters, downtown, at the University of Guelph and across the city. Each icon shows the kind of rack, and the number shows how many bikes it holds.

More than 500 locations inside city limits, from single bollards to covered shelters.

Where the data comes from

Everything on this map comes from OpenStreetMap, the free map anyone can edit. The map checks for changes every week.

Something missing or wrong?

Tap the spot and choose Details / fix to correct it in OpenStreetMap, or add new parking. Not a mapper? Report it here. Adding the number of spaces helps most.
